About Us

Welcome to Ryu Hakone Steak House

At Ryu Hakone, we bring together the comforting flavors of Japanese and Filipino cuisine. Our restaurant serves special set meals that combine the best of both worlds, giving you a unique and satisfying dining experience. Located in the beautiful area of Hakone, we are dedicated to serving delicious food made with care and offering a warm, welcoming space for all our guests. Whether you are looking for a taste of traditional Japanese hospitality or the heartwarming flavors of the Philippines, our set meals are crafted to make you feel right at home. Come and share a delicious meal with us!

VISION & MISSION

Vision

To be the most loved restaurant in Hakone.

To be the most loved restaurant in Hakone where families and friends connect over delicious, homemade Japanese and Filipino set meals.

Mission

To serve valued visitors with premium beef, craft, and warmth.

At Ryu Hakone Restaurant, we serve locals and travelers in Hakone with delicious Japanese and Filipino set meals. By using modern kitchen technology to ensure fast, safe, and high-quality service, we keep our business strong and growing. We believe in sharing warm Japanese hospitality and friendly Filipino service. Our special mix of flavors is what makes us stand out. We care for our community by using good, local ingredients, and we treat our team with love and respect so they can give you the best experience.
